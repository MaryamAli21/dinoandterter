A little courage. A wonderful story.

Dino loves to play, but trying something new can feel scary. When he meets Terter, a kind little turtle, he discovers that being brave doesn't mean never feeling afraid — sometimes all it takes is one small step.

A gentle message

A reassuring story about confidence, friendship, and the courage to try again.

Made for sharing

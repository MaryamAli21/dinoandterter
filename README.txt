Dino and Terter ready-to-use website

Open index.html directly in your browser. The book cover is embedded in this HTML, so it displays even without the assets folder.

When publishing the website at https://www.dinoandterter.com/, keep assets/book-cover.webp next to index.html under the assets/ folder. This image is referenced by Open Graph/Twitter share preview meta tags. The visible hero cover is already embedded in index.html.

Contact and Subscribe use mailto: rather than a backend. They require a configured email app and the visitor must press Send. For automatic signup storage, integrate a mailing-list provider or backend later.
